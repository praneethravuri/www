## Prompt

Create a wide landscape 2:1 illustration for the Tether software portfolio project: exactly TWO clearly recognizable desktop computers talking to each other. Each computer has a slim rectangular monitor on a small stand and a simple keyboard, facing inward toward the other computer in precise orthographic 30-degree isometric view. Connect the two computers with a clear orange line, with two small angular outlined message envelopes traveling in opposite directions along it, making two-way communication immediately obvious. Art direction is CAD technical drafting, geometric engineering drawing, NOT cartoon, NOT playful 3D rendering. Fine crisp white and neutral gray outlines, minimal flat charcoal plane fills, restrained vivid orange #FF5D20 for the connection and message outlines. Screens show only a few simple abstract terminal lines in white and orange; NO readable words, NO letters, NO numbers. Perfectly flat solid background #151414. Center the compact composition with generous margin on every side, especially top and bottom. No gradients, no glow, no soft lighting, no textures, no characters, no faces, no robots, no extra computers, no decorative grids, no logos, no watermarks. Technical line art must remain legible when displayed 720 pixels wide. The subject must read unmistakably as two computers exchanging messages.
